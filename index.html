    Situations we help with

    Clients tend to call us at similar moments. Here are a few examples drawn from our team's experience. Client details are always kept confidential.

    An investor needs confidence before signing

    We assess the target's operations, technology and cost structure, flag the risks and size the standalone costs, so the deal team knows what it is buying.

    A sponsor needs to find value in a portfolio company

    We review cost, operations and technology, then rank the opportunities by impact and ease of execution. Leadership knows where to act first.

    A portfolio company needs hands-on leadership

    We step in alongside management to run critical programs, from the 100-day plan to transformation, and keep the sponsor informed along the way.

    A business is being carved out or spun off

    We plan and run the separation, from transition services to standalone setup, so operations continue without disruption on Day 1.

    Two companies are coming together

    We set up and run the integration, track synergies against the deal case, and help people and leadership through the change.

    A company is bringing new products to market

    We define who the products are for and how to position them, then build the launch plan and the tools sales teams need to sell with confidence.

    How an engagement runs

    Every engagement is shaped to the situation, but most follow the same arc. You can bring us in at any stage.

    1. Diagnose

      We get under the numbers fast: where value sits, where risk sits, and what has to be true for the deal or program to work.

    2. Design

      We turn the diagnosis into a plan with clear owners, milestones and a target operating model your leadership can stand behind.

    3. Execute

      We run the program alongside your team, manage the moving parts across functions and keep decisions moving.

    4. Hand over

      We leave your people able to run what we built, with the tools and reporting to keep it on track.
