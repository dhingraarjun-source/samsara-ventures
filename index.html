What we do

Three kinds of work, all built around the same question: what does this business need to look like on the other side of the deal, and how do we get it there without losing value along the way?

Divestitures and carve-outs

Separating a business unit cleanly, so the seller keeps running smoothly and the buyer gets something that works on day one.

  • Separation planning and governance
  • Transition service agreements
  • Stranded cost reduction
  • Day one readiness

Mergers and integration

Bringing two organizations together with a clear plan for people, systems and the synergies that justified the deal.

  • Pre-close diligence support
  • Integration management office
  • Synergy tracking
  • Operating model design

Cost and operating transformation

Finding where cost and complexity have built up, and reshaping the business to grow profitably under market pressure.

  • Cost structure diagnostics
  • Footprint and back-office review
  • Technology rationalization
  • Revenue quality analysis

How an engagement runs

Every engagement is shaped to the situation, but most follow the same arc. You can bring us in at any stage.

  1. Diagnose

    We get under the numbers fast: where value sits, where risk sits, and what has to be true for the deal or program to work.

  2. Design

    We turn the diagnosis into a plan with clear owners, milestones and a target operating model your leadership can stand behind.

  3. Execute

    We run the program alongside your team, manage the moving parts across functions and keep decisions moving.

  4. Hand over

    We leave your people able to run what we built, with the tools and reporting to keep it on track.

Why Samsara

In Sanskrit, samsara describes the continuous cycle of change and renewal.

Companies go through their own version of it. Units are formed, combined, separated and reborn as something new. We think those transitions are where the most value is created or lost, and that is where we focus.
